For You

A daily selection.
Not a pile.

In For You, the Unread view shows at most 30 reads at a time, picked from the last 48 hours and sorted into three groups: Just In, Today, You may have missed. The tab badge counts that selection, never the volume. The engine learns what matters to you, on the device, and follows what you read lately.

  • The Daily Brief: the topics several of your sources are covering, usually three, with their articles
  • With Apple Intelligence, a summary paragraph generated on the device; without it, the Daily Brief is still there
  • A morning notification at the time you choose: “3 topics this morning”
  • “Not interested”, “This doesn't belong here”, “Exclude from brief”: your corrections, on all your devices

Privacy

Your reading is nobody's business but yours.

Everything stays with you

Your content and reading activity stay on your devices and in your personal iCloud. Ranking, topic grouping and summaries are computed on the device.

Nobody profiles you

No ads, no ad profile, no advertising or tracking SDK, no account to create. You are not the product.

Your emails, only read

Gmail read-only, processed on the device, never stored elsewhere. TechnoDaily has passed the independent security audit required by Google (CASA).

A private sync

Between your devices, through your personal iCloud, encrypted by Apple. We have no access to it.

A screen, “What the app has learned”, shows what the engine took from your reading, and what it does not learn.

And usage statistics? Anonymous, aggregated, off by default. You decide. Read the privacy policy →
